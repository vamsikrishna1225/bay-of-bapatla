BAPATLA, ANDHRA PRADESH

Bapatla Beach

A peaceful coastal escape for sea views, sunrise moments, relaxed beach walks and a taste of the Bay of Bengal.

About Bapatla Beach

Bapatla Beach is one of the coastal attractions to explore around Bapatla. It is a good place to slow down, enjoy the sea breeze and watch the changing colours of the coast.

Combine a beach visit with nearby heritage and food experiences to create a relaxed Bapatla day trip.

What makes it special?

The open shoreline, coastal atmosphere and sunrise views make Bapatla Beach a scenic stop for travellers exploring the Bay of Bapatla.

Things To Do at Bapatla Beach

Keep it simple, scenic and coastal.

🌅 Sunrise Watching

Start early for cooler weather, softer light and beautiful sunrise photographs.

🚶 Beach Walks

Take a relaxed walk along the shore and enjoy the sea breeze.

📷 Photography

Capture the shoreline, sky, waves and everyday coastal life.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Time

A simple destination for spending unhurried time by the coast.

🍴 Local Food

Explore Bapatla for local Andhra and coastal food after your beach visit.

🛕 Heritage Visit

Pair the beach with a visit to Bhavanarayana Swamy Temple.
